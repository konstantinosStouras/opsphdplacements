# Stage 1 title-scope correction — 25 October 2026

The owner narrowed the public register to universities with a relevant doctoral title or named academic unit: Operations, Operations Research, Operations Management, Management Science or Industrial Engineering. Industrial and Systems Engineering is retained as an Industrial Engineering title; other engineering fields and broad logistics/supply-chain/decision-science/optimization research topics alone do not qualify. A named specialization within a broader PhD qualifies when the official source presents it as a doctoral research area. This is a conservative title gate, not a finding that excluded universities have no relevant scholars or research.

The pending three-school batch added four officially evidenced programme records: Colorado School of Mines Operations Research with Engineering PhD, Binghamton Industrial and Systems Engineering PhD, and NOVA University Lisbon Management PhD Operations/Technology/Innovation area plus Industrial Engineering PhD. Colorado School of Mines qualifies by its PhD title; its participating Mechanical Engineering, Economics and Business, Minerals, Applied Mathematics and Computer Science units are not labelled as qualifying Ops/IE departments. The same display rule is applied to all reviewed university units and original directory labels. Programme host school/department names appear only when they meet the title gate. Internal source facts remain in the 422-record research log and original imports; they are not used in qualifying public counts or department display.
