Abstract

This project tracks PhD careers in Operations Research, Operations Management and Industrial Engineering. The first edition brings together 2,149 faculty profiles from OM List, across 202 institutions, with source-reported doctoral education and affiliations. First placements are researched from university reports and CVs, with direct evidence links and an explicit record of outstanding research.

Keywords: doctoral education; academic careers; operations; industrial engineering.

05

Sources and methodology

A transparent record of what the evidence says—and what remains unknown.

5.1

Scope and collection

Our scope is Operations Research, Operations Management and Industrial Engineering. The initial faculty source is the OM List faculty directory. All 2,149 profiles from its 16 October 2026 faculty snapshot are included, with doctorate information matched by source profile URL. The source excludes Industrial Engineering departments and does not establish the discipline of every person's PhD.

5.2

Appointments versus placements

A listed faculty appointment is kept separate from a verified first placement. PhD institution and year are retained only where supplied by the source. Missing information remains unknown.

5.3

Verification and provenance

Imported records retain their source link and retrieval date. A verified first placement requires an explicit initial-placement report or a documented employment chronology after the doctorate. A postdoc before a faculty job is the first placement. A cohort year is not assumed to be the job start year; unreported roles and dates stay unknown. Ambiguous timelines remain unresolved.

5.4

Updates and corrections

The repository records changes to each published dataset. Download the current selection as CSV, inspect the source, or report a correction or removal request. Do not include private information in a public issue.

Coverage limitations and interpretation

This is a directory-based view, not a complete census of PhD graduates or jobs. Faculty outside the source, industry careers, and unreported appointments may be absent. Cohort counts describe only faculty with a known PhD year. They should not be interpreted as graduating class sizes or employment rates. Source classifications are not independently verified degree specializations.

Graduate census and industry careers

The graduate census will begin with a historical register of Operations Management, Operations Research and Industrial Engineering doctoral programs, including closed and renamed programs. We will enumerate graduates by program and year regardless of career destination, then add employment histories, doctoral advisors and publications through the PhD year. Each fact will carry its own sources and last-checked date. Industry entry and later moves out of academia will be recorded as separate career events. Publications through the PhD calendar year will be distinguished from publications known to precede the actual degree date. See the research design and dataset structure. Current coverage remains the imported faculty directory and its reviewed first placements.

Privacy and source attribution

This site republishes public professional information: names, institutional affiliations, academic roles, and PhD information where available. It does not publish personal contact details. No advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies are added by this application. Hosting providers may maintain operational logs.
